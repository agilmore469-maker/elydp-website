About Us

Built by dog owners, for the whole town

The Ely Dog Park sits on city-owned land at 2100 Old Airport Road, right beside the Ely softball fields on the east end of town. The grounds are leased to the Ely Dog Park Association, a local 501(c)(3) nonprofit made up of Ely dog owners.

The fenced area includes a separate small-dog section, poop stations, benches, a water station, and a shelter, all maintained by volunteers so the park stays safe and welcoming year-round.

Where we are: On the east end of Ely, just off Highway 1 on Old Airport Road, next to the Ely softball fields.

Who runs it: The Ely Dog Park Association, an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit, in partnership with the City of Ely.

Hours: Open dawn to dusk, every day. Dogs must be at least 6 months old and wear a current license or rabies tag.

Cost: Free to visit — the Association raises its maintenance funds through donations.

Mission Statement

What we stand for

Our purpose is to establish a fenced in, off-leash Dog Park where well-behaved canine citizens can exercise in a clean, safe environment without endangering or annoying people, property, or wildlife. To develop a beautiful well maintained space open to all dog lovers and friends who are willing to uphold the park rules, restrictions, and educate the public. To view this park as a community project, in partnership with the city of Ely, designed to satisfy the needs of dog owners and non-dog owners alike.

Park Rules

Keep it safe & friendly for every dog

  • Park hours are dawn to dusk, and all posted conditions of the park's Conditional Use Permit apply.
  • Dogs must be at least 6 months old and visibly wearing a current City of Ely pet license tag (a visible rabies tag is acceptable for visitors from outside Ely).
  • Dogs known to be aggressive, females in heat, or animals classified as dangerous under city or state law are not permitted in the park.
  • Pinch, prong, and spike collars aren't allowed inside the park.
  • Owners must stay in control of and keep sight of their dog(s) at all times, and must carry a leash at all times, even in the off-leash area. Dogs must be leashed outside the fenced park.
  • Owners must clean up after their dogs and dispose of waste in the containers provided.
  • Dog owners are legally and financially responsible for their dog's behavior; if a dog injures another dog or person, the owner must exchange name and contact information before leaving.
  • Food, glass containers, alcohol, tobacco, and cannabis products are prohibited, and motor vehicles aren't allowed beyond approved maintenance vehicles.
  • Dogs are not permitted on the neighboring softball/baseball fields — the fenced dog park only.
  • The City of Ely and the Ely Dog Park Association aren't responsible for supervising dogs or for any damage or injury caused by a dog; visiting the park is at your own risk. Report rule violations to the Association, and call 911 for emergencies.
